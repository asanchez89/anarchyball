# Third-party assets

Only assets actively integrated into the game are copied into the curated
`assets/art/` directory. Original source packs remain
local and are ignored by Git so the repository does not redistribute them as
standalone asset collections.

## Press Start 2P

- Integrated files: `assets/fonts/press_start_2p/PressStart2P-Regular.ttf`,
  `OFL.txt` and `FONTLOG.txt`.
- Source: Google Fonts, `ofl/pressstart2p`.
- Author: Cody Boisclair / CodeMan38, with later expansion by Cyreal.
- License: SIL Open Font License 1.1; the complete license is stored beside
  the font.

## Warped Collection

- Creator: Luis Zuno, also known as Ansimuz (`@ansimuz`).
- Integrated selection: Twilight Forest background layers, terrain tiles,
  large trees and vegetation; Props Pack machinery, checkpoint capsule and
  collectible power-ups used across World 0; the three-frame Caves gate used
  for encounter barriers; plus the two-frame Warped Station missile used by
  the player's defensive projectile.
- Terms detected in the bundled license: permitted in personal and commercial
  projects and may be modified; redistribution or resale as standalone assets
  is not permitted.
- License copy: `assets/licenses/ansimuz_warped_collection_license.pdf`.

## Pixel-art VFX packs

The local VFX source packs did not include a detectable license or terms file
at review time. No VFX from those packs is integrated until its usage terms can
be verified.

## SciFi Sound Design Pack — Volume 1

- Creator: Clint Wagoner.
- Integrated selection: fifteen renamed WAV files under `assets/audio/sfx/world_0/`
  for movement/combat feedback and World 0 interaction, progress and completion events.
- Terms detected in the bundled license: royalty-free use and modification in
  personal and commercial projects; raw redistribution and AI/ML use are
  prohibited.
- License copy: `assets/licenses/scifi_sound_design_pack_v1_license.txt`.

The local Pixel Combat sound pack has no detectable license file and remains
unintegrated until its usage terms can be verified.
