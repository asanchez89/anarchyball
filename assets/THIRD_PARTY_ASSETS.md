# Third-party assets

Only assets actively integrated into the game are copied into the curated
`assets/art/` directory. Original source packs remain
local and are ignored by Git so the repository does not redistribute them as
standalone asset collections.

## Press Start 2P

- Integrated files: `assets/fonts/press_start_2p/PressStart2P-Regular.ttf`,
  `OFL.txt` and `FONTLOG.txt`.
- Source: Google Fonts, `ofl/pressstart2p`.
- Author: Cody Boisclair / CodeMan38, with later expansion by Cyreal.
- License: SIL Open Font License 1.1; the complete license is stored beside
  the font.

## Warped Collection

- Workshop inventory icons: `assets/art/props/world_0/workshop_items.png`, copied
  unchanged from Props Pack 1 / 32x32 Icons / Scifi weapons and items.png. Runtime
  atlas regions show ammunition, service components, an electronic keycard and
  the two player weapons. Source pixels are unchanged.

- Creator: Luis Zuno, also known as Ansimuz (`@ansimuz`).
- Integrated selection: Twilight Forest background layers, terrain tiles,
  large trees and vegetation; Props Pack machinery, checkpoint capsule and
  collectible power-ups used across World 0; the three-frame Caves gate used
  for encounter barriers; plus the two-frame Warped Station missile used by
  the player's defensive projectile.
- Terms detected in the bundled license: permitted in personal and commercial
  projects and may be modified; redistribution or resale as standalone assets
  is not permitted.
- License copy: `assets/licenses/ansimuz_warped_collection_license.pdf`.
- Workshop scenery adds unmodified Props Pack 2 / Set 1 sprites `box-1.png`
  and `container-small-gray.png`, curated as `workshop_crate.png` and
  `workshop_container.png`. Authored placements live in `workshop_layout.json`.

The workshop industrial pass additionally uses unmodified Warped
`sci-fi-interior-platform/PNG/background.png`,
`tile-set-sci-fi-interior-platform.png`, its preview, and
`cyberpunk-corridor-files/Props/PNG/elevator-track.png` under
`assets/art/world_0/workshop_industrial/`. Atlas regions are selected at runtime;
no purchased art was submitted to image generation. Metal deck plates, braced
columns and elevator guides replace Twilight Forest ruins in the workshop only.

## Pixel-art VFX packs

The local VFX source packs did not include a detectable license or terms file
at review time. No VFX from those packs is integrated until its usage terms can
be verified.

## SciFi Sound Design Pack — Volume 1

- Creator: Clint Wagoner.
- Integrated selection: fifteen renamed WAV files under `assets/audio/sfx/world_0/`
  for movement/combat feedback and World 0 interaction, progress and completion events.
- Terms detected in the bundled license: royalty-free use and modification in
  personal and commercial projects; raw redistribution and AI/ML use are
  prohibited.
- License copy: `assets/licenses/scifi_sound_design_pack_v1_license.txt`.

Additional gameplay derivative: `tactical_dash.wav`, a 0.32-second faded mono
excerpt of `SciFi-V1_SpaceShips_18-FlyBy14_24b-48k.wav` for relay movement.
Prepared by a deterministic local trim, not generative audio or model training.

The local Pixel Combat sound pack has no detectable license file and remains
unintegrated until its usage terms can be verified.

Workshop dressing uses two unchanged Warped Collection cyberpunk-corridor PNGs:
`small-terminal.png` copied as `bitcoin_atm_terminal.png`, and
`server-gabinetes.png` copied as `workshop_servers.png`, under `assets/art/props/world_0`.
The BTC label is rendered separately in-game. Covered by the existing Ansimuz
Warped Collection license; no image generation or model training was used.

`assets/audio/sfx/world_0/generated/theft_retro.wav` is an original deterministic
chiptone; `contact_hit_retro.wav` is a separate original low descending impact
for contact damage, generated by the same script without external recordings.
The recovery package reuses the licensed Warped `workshop_crate.png` art.

The theft sound is a
descending chiptone, synthesized by `tools/audio/generate_retro_cues.ps1 -TheftOnly`.
The short pixel-wave theft effect is code-rendered (`src/presentation/theft_burst.gd`);
neither asset copies external pack content.
